Traeco

A quality-aware recommendation engine that helps teams cut AI agent costs without sacrificing output quality.

Live Site ↗ App ↗ traeco-sdk on PyPI ↗
Role Co-Founder & Chief AI Officer
Team 4 — CEO, CTO, CPO, CAIO (me)
Dates November 2025 – Present

The Problem

Teams deploying LLM agents have no reliable way to tell which model or prompt choice is wasting money versus actually necessary for quality — cost and quality live in separate dashboards, if they're tracked at all.

What I Built

Co-built the core web app foundation with one other developer — the agent spend-breakdown dashboard and surrounding platform. On top of that, I specifically built:

  • An LLM-judge-scored recommendation engine (faithfulness and structure-conformance checks) that surfaces cost-saving model-switch and prompt optimizations.
  • A custom eval-cluster framework for benchmarking agent quality across teams.
  • Multi-user infrastructure with invite and role/permission flows.
  • The published traeco-sdk (PyPI) for instrumenting OpenAI and Anthropic calls.
  • A custom n8n community node so no-code workflows can report cost/token data in.

Outcome & Impact

  • Top 10% of applicants, YC Summer 2026 batch.
  • In active conversations with design partners — including Amazon's Ring — on integrating Traeco's cost/quality monitoring into their AI agent workflows.
  • SDK published and live on PyPI.
