SwarmLight

A physics-driven 3D puzzle game where you control a swarm of fireflies, collect objects, and manage your swarm's energy.

Play on itch.io ↗ Gameplay Video ↗
Role Lead Developer
Team 2 — me (systems/graphics), partner (level/puzzle design)
Dates September 2025 – May 2026

The Problem

We wanted to develop a game that was puzzle based but included unique mechanics and a unique player character that hasn't been seen before. Players needed a way to physically manipulate objects using a swarm of independent agents that still felt alive and organic rather than mechanical — grabbing solid objects, entering hollow ones, and managing a shared energy resource across the whole swarm.

What I Built

I owned swarm physics and movement, pickup mechanics, all graphics programming, frog enemy mechanics, and telemetry for user research; my partner focused on game and level design with an emphasis on puzzle mechanics.

  • Dual pickup modes — LMB clusters the swarm around solid objects to carry them via "squeeze," RMB spreads fireflies inside hollow objects via "possess."
  • Flocking movement built on cohesion forces and MovePosition rather than direct velocity, for smoother formation behavior.
  • ClosestPoint()-based distribution so fireflies wrap evenly around a surface instead of clumping on one side.
  • Frog enemy mechanics that sense the swarm within a certain radius, stretch their tongue out, and kill any fireflies that collide.
  • A full energy-absorption/revival loop instrumented with a custom telemetry framework — tracking level time, absorption count, revival count, total energy absorbed, and deaths for user research.

Outcome & Impact

  • Diagnosed and fixed a core economy bug where drain outpaced gain at scale (40 fireflies), rebalancing absorption rate and total energy pool to make the swarm sustainable.
  • Gained rewarding playtest feedback that the game is enjoyable, emotional, and intense — meeting our experience goals.
  • Met all GDLC deadlines and successfully published on itch.io.
