Adaptive Pursuit

A procedural UE5 parkour-pursuit game where an AI enemy adapts to your specific movement patterns in real time.

In Development
Role Solo Developer
Team 1 — everything, end to end
Dates 2026 — In Development

The Problem

Scripted enemy AI in parkour/pursuit games becomes predictable and exploitable once a player learns its patterns — the goal was an opponent that actually adapts to the individual player instead of running a fixed behavior tree.

What I Built

  • A custom PyTorch PPO agent — built from scratch, no stable-baselines3 — governing pursuit behavior.
  • An LSTM model that trains on the player's live movement during the session to predict their trajectory, feeding a runtime interception planner that runs route-graph search against those predictions.
  • When initial training produced a degenerate policy (reward stuck at -3.9M with zero gradient), diagnosed and fixed it through reward scaling, an advantage-normalization epsilon fix, and input-state normalization to resolve entropy collapse in the policy network.
  • Player movement mechanics in UE5/C++ — keyboard-controlled traversal with mouse-click grapple integration.

Outcome & Impact

The fixed training run completed 3M steps on CPU, with reward crossing from deeply negative to a best episode reward of +15.03 — producing a viable, working checkpoint. Currently developing the player mechanics further and tuning the enemy intelligence.
